📖 Project Storyline

Scene 1 — The Opportunity
A Retailer Drowning in Data, Starving for Insight
NovaSpark Retail — a fast-growing omni-channel consumer-electronics brand with 420 stores across 12 countries — was sitting on 18 months of raw transaction data but had no unified way to read it. Revenue leakage was invisible. Stockouts were reactive. The company's 2,400 SKUs had never been ranked by true profitability. The sales force worked blind, with no daily signals telling them which customers to call, which products to push, or which regions to protect.
Scene 2 — The Intelligence Layer
Fusing Data Streams into a Single Command Centre
The data science team fused point-of-sale records, e-commerce clickstreams, CRM loyalty events, supply-chain lead times, and external macro signals (inflation index, consumer sentiment) into one Microsoft Fabric OneLake lakehouse. Machine-learning models scored every customer on churn risk, upsell probability, and lifetime value — giving the sales force a daily "next-best-action" queue for the first time. Real-time dashboards replaced static spreadsheets.
Scene 3 — The Results
Award-Winning Outcomes in Under Two Quarters
Within two quarters, NovaSpark achieved a 23% lift in revenue-per-visitor, reduced stockouts by 41%, and cut promotional over-spend by $4.2M. Repeat purchase rate climbed to 63.8% (+7.1 pp) and NPS hit 72 (up 9 points). The platform earned the Gartner Retail Analytics Excellence Award 2024 and became a Microsoft Fabric OneLake reference architecture showcase — a model for the entire industry.

⚠️ Business Problem Statements

🔴 Problem 01 — Revenue Intelligence
💸
Hidden Revenue Leakage Across 2,400 SKUs
NovaSpark had no single view of which SKUs were truly profitable after accounting for returns, discounts, and COGS. Gross margin ranged from 38% to 62% across the catalogue, but managers were making ranging decisions on revenue alone — not profit. Estimated loss: $12–15M annually in suboptimal promotions and markdowns.
Revenue Leakage · SKU Profitability
🔴 Problem 02 — Demand Forecasting
📦
Reactive Inventory Causing Stockouts & Overstock
Inventory decisions were based on last-month's sales, ignoring seasonality, campaign signals, and macro trends. Gaming and Smartphones saw demand surges of 60–90% in Q4, yet replenishment was triggered only after stockout events. The cost: lost sales, emergency freight, and $3.8M in excess slow-moving inventory elsewhere.
Demand Planning · Supply Chain
🔴 Problem 03 — Customer Intelligence
👥
11.2% Churn Rate with No Early-Warning System
Customers were churning silently — no model existed to flag at-risk accounts before they left. Enterprise B2B and Premium Consumer segments, which drove 67% of gross profit, were the highest-value targets but had no dedicated retention triggers. Sales reps had no next-best-action guidance, leading to reactive rather than proactive engagement.
Customer Retention · CLV Scoring
🔴 Problem 04 — Omni-Channel Visibility
🌏
Siloed Channel Data Across 5 Commerce Touchpoints
Physical Store, E-Commerce, Mobile App, Partner Marketplace, and Click & Collect each ran separate analytics stacks. There was no unified conversion funnel, no cross-channel basket attribution, and no way to compare Campaign ROI (ranging from 1.8x to 4.5x) across regions. Promotional budgets were allocated on gut feel rather than signal.
Omni-Channel · Attribution

✅ Proposed AI-Powered Solutions

🟢 Solution 01 — SKU Profit Intelligence
🧠
AI-Driven True-Profitability Ranking Engine
Deploy a generative AI layer (Claude API) that ingests GP margin, return rate, discount depth, and velocity data per SKU. It produces daily natural-language summaries — "iPhone 15 Pro is your highest-margin Star SKU at 54.4% GP; consider reducing its discount tier from 15% to 10% in APAC." Managers query the AI directly without touching raw data. Expected uplift: $4–6M gross profit recovery.
Claude API · Generative AI · Profit Ops
🟢 Solution 02 — Predictive Demand Forecasting
🔮
8-Week Ahead Demand Signals with AI Alerts
ML models trained on POS history, clickstream intent, macro signals, and seasonality generate 8-week demand forecasts per SKU per region. Integrated with an AI alert engine (Claude + Apps Script) that sends Slack/email notifications: "Stockout risk: PlayStation 5 in APAC will hit zero in 12 days at current sell-through — reorder 340 units." Stockout reduction target: −41% (achieved).
ML Forecasting · Real-Time Alerts
🟢 Solution 03 — Customer Churn Prevention AI
🎯
Next-Best-Action Queue Powered by CLV Scoring
Every customer is scored daily on churn probability, upsell propensity, and lifetime value. A generative AI assistant translates scores into actionable scripts for reps: "CUST-24592 (Enterprise B2B, Australia) has a 78% churn probability — last purchase was 47 days ago. Recommend outreach with a loyalty tier upgrade offer." Repeat purchase rate lifted from 56.7% → 63.8% in two quarters.
CLV · Churn AI · Next-Best-Action
🟢 Solution 04 — Unified Channel Intelligence
⚡
Single Lakehouse View + AI Report Generator
All five channels feed into one Microsoft Fabric OneLake lakehouse with a unified data model. An AI report generator (Claude API) produces weekly executive briefings automatically: cross-channel conversion comparisons, campaign ROI rankings, basket-size anomalies, and regional performance gaps — in plain English, ready to present in 60 seconds. Promo over-spend saved: $4.2M.
OneLake · Fabric · Auto Reports
